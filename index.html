See what you’ll learn

The first five sounds you’ll learn. Tap one to hear it.

From sounds to stories

Twelve units and 48 short lessons, each built around clear Hindi audio. Every unit ends with a checkpoint, so you know when you’re ready to move on.

Every letter, one tap away

The character chart keeps all 56 vowels and consonants close. Tap any of them to hear its sound and see which ones you’ve already learned.

Trace every letter

Follow the guide over each Devanagari character at your own pace. Undo, clear and go again. There’s no handwriting score, just practice.

Say it out loud

Record yourself and listen back. Many words get pronunciation feedback from a model that runs on your device; for the rest, you compare your recording with the example. Recordings are deleted when your practice session ends.

Read your first stories

Short stories and conversations written for beginners. Listen to the whole story, or tap any line to see its English meaning.

Phrases for real life

Greetings, family, friends, food and getting around. Every phrase has audio, so you can hear it before you try it.

Made for families, private by design

A learner for everyone at home

  • Each learner keeps their own progress, streak and settings.
  • No account to create, and no ads.
  • Larger text, dark appearance, reduced motion, and instructions read aloud for early readers.
  • Achievements for milestones, plus an optional Game Center leaderboard for one linked learner.

Your voice stays on your device

  • Speaking practice is checked on your iPhone or iPad. Recordings are never uploaded.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no sign-in.
  • Download the course once and keep learning offline.
  • The privacy policy explains exactly what the app stores.
